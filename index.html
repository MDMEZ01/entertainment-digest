Today's Deep Dive

Anthropic Breaks Its IPO Floor

Why it matters
  • Anthropic traded at $80.02 early Tuesday, down 9% and below its $83 IPO price on its fourth trading day, after a Monday close of $87.64.
  • The stock has fallen about 30% from its $115.10 debut-day high, and Monday's record Nvidia rally did nothing to lift it.
  • Form 4s filed Monday show Dario and Daniela Amodei each sold 3,378,378 shares at $83 (about $561M combined); all seven co-founders sold roughly $1.96B.
The money
  • Analysts launched coverage bullishly Tuesday: Canaccord at Buy with a $135 target, Oppenheimer at Outperform with $150, and Wedbush at Outperform with $120.
  • Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley each committed to 200.9M shares at $83, about $16.67B apiece, and Google and Nvidia took $10B each in the private placement.
Historical context
  • Facebook's 2012 IPO broke its $38 offer price on its second trading day, and the stock did not reclaim it for more than a year.
Watch for
  • MSCI adds the stock to its indexes after the Nov 30 close, a test of whether index buying can steady the price.
  • Whether the selloff in AI infrastructure names, with CoreWeave and Nebius each down 8% Tuesday, keeps pressure on the $83 line.
