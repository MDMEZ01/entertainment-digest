📈 Today's Deep Dive

The AI Build-Out Meets 5% Yields: Oracle's Stargate Force Majeure Is the Warning Shot

Why it matters
  • Oracle filed an unusual force majeure claim on its $165B, 1,400-acre New Mexico Stargate campus with SoftBank and OpenAI, citing permitting delays (Puck).
  • About $18B of construction loans from banks including Jefferies and Santander already trade below par, and Blue Owl funds put in $3B of equity.
  • The 10-year Treasury near 5.17%, its highest since 2007, raises the cost of every debt-funded data center on the drawing board (CNBC).
The money
  • JPMorgan sees AI-related debt reaching $4.1 trillion by 2030; SoftBank's $11.1B junk bond sale carried yields up to 9.75%.
  • Oracle stock is down 52% in a year with roughly $125B of debt "on the BBB cliff"; CoreWeave's interest bill rises about $30M per point of rates.
Historical context
  • The 1990s fiber build-out was debt-financed on demand forecasts that arrived late, and it ended in the 2001–02 collapses of Global Crossing and WorldCom.
Watch for
  • Whether Oracle renegotiates or withholds lease payments if the site misses its 2028 opening.
  • Local pushback: 69% of Americans oppose a data center near them in an NBC News/SurveyMonkey poll.
