📈 Today's Deep Dive

Disney Re-Opens the Vault to Netflix: The Streaming War's New Truce

Why it matters
  • Disney is licensing current hits to Netflix globally, starting today with "Percy Jackson and the Olympians" Seasons 1–2 and all five "Ice Age" films.
  • The windows are promotional: "Percy Jackson" Season 3 premieres on Disney+ Nov. 20, and "Ice Age: Boiling Point" hits theaters in February 2027.
  • The two-year slate goes deep, with "Will Trent" Seasons 1–4 (Nov. 3), "Shifting Gears" (Dec. 1, U.S.) and "Tracker" (Feb. 26, 2027, international).
The money
  • Terms were not disclosed, but the deal brings licensing revenue as Disney's Josh D'Amaro and Hugh Johnston say they are "highly focused on reducing costs" amid layoffs.
  • Netflix gets proven titles while Ted Sarandos admits viewing is "not growing as fast as I want."
Historical context
  • Disney ended its Netflix pay-TV output deal with its 2019 releases to stock Disney+; Pixar's "Soul," "Elio" and "Raya and the Last Dragon" arriving in early 2027 reverses part of that.
Watch for
  • Whether "Percy Jackson" Season 3 gets a lift on Disney+ from Netflix sampling, the test of licensing as marketing.
  • Whether Skydance, after Tuesday's close, licenses HBO and Paramount titles the same way while it pays down debt.

7.3 Million: NBC's Record MLB Wild Card Return

The figure
  • Red Sox–Yankees Wild Card Game 1 drew 7.3M viewers on NBC and Peacock (Front Office Sports, Oct. 2).
Context
  • It was the most-watched Game 1 of any Wild Card or Division Series on any network since 2006, and up 12% from the same matchup on ESPN in 2025.
