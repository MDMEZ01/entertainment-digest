📈 Today’s Deep Dive

Zaz's Secret Plan B: Could WBD Merge With NBCU Instead of Paramount?

Why It Matters
  • Puck's William Cohan reports a scenario where WBD's Streaming and Studios business could eventually combine with NBCU — spun off from Comcast as the $5B public company Versant — instead of completing the Paramount deal.
  • The scenario would only become viable after June 4, 2027, the date Ellison can walk away and pay WBD a $7B breakup fee, meaning Zaslav has a real off-ramp if the antitrust trial or financing environment sours.
  • It reframes the Paramount deal not as a done pending formality but as one of several live outcomes Wall Street is now pricing in, alongside WBD stock trading 20% below the deal's takeout price.
The Money
  • Larry Ellison has personally agreed to underwrite the Paramount deal with a $45.7B check, but his net worth has fallen more than $66B year-to-date to $181B, while Oracle now carries $130B in debt rated just above junk.
  • Any NBCU-WBD combination would need to wait two years post-spin to merge tax-free, the same structural constraint that shaped the original Comcast-Versant spinoff.
Historical Context
  • Cohan says he first discussed this exact NBCU-WBD combination with CNBC co-founder Tom Rogers back in August 2022 — meaning the idea predates the current Paramount bid by three years and reflects long-standing thinking about the endpoint of legacy-media consolidation.
Watch For
  • Whether WBD's Cavanagh, positioned as a potential future CEO successor, becomes the linchpin of a Plan B combination if Zaslav, then 69, retires around the same timeline.
  • Whether Oracle's rising debt load and falling credit quality forces Ellison's hand before the March 2027 trial even concludes.
