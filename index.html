📈 Today's Deep Dive

The $44B Bill for WarnerMount: Paramount Borrows Big as Yields Hit a 19-Year High

Why it matters
  • Paramount launched a $44.4B bond sale ($32B investment-grade, $12.4B high-yield) to fund its Warner Bros. Discovery takeover, on top of a $7.5B loan launched the week before.
  • The combined company would carry about $80B of debt, and Puck's Dylan Byers flags "the impending layoffs it will necessitate" as Ellison promises $6B in annual savings with no specifics.
  • WBD shareholders are due $31 a share, so the debt load, not the equity, now decides how much of Warner survives intact.
The money
  • Price talk on the 10-year dollar notes came in "in the low 9% area," and Bloomberg reports enough demand for yields up to 9%; Skydance hopes to price by Wednesday.
  • The Wakeup, citing Deadline and Moody's, puts first-year interest above $6B (about 60% of the pair's FY25 adjusted EBITDA) and opening leverage near 7x, versus a 3.75x target by end-2028.
Historical context
  • AT&T's $85B purchase of Time Warner in 2018 left it with roughly $180B of debt; four years later it unwound the deal by spinning WarnerMedia into Discovery, creating the very company Paramount is now buying.
Watch for
  • Judge Araceli Martínez-Olguín's ruling on the 12-state settlement; Paramount estimates an Oct. 7 close but its SEC filing says the date "is uncertain."
  • Final bond pricing this week and the Class B share move from Nasdaq to the NYSE, set for Oct. 6.
