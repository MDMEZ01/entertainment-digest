📈 Today's Deep Dive

The Blue Wave Hands Congress a Subpoena Pen Over Media and AI

Why it matters
  • Democrats won both chambers on Nov. 3, and Puck's Eriq Gardner flags earlier letters to David Ellison, Amazon's Andy Jassy, Nexstar and EA as likely oversight targets.
  • Probable chairs include Jamie Raskin at Judiciary, Frank Pallone at Energy and Commerce, Robert Garcia at Oversight and Richard Blumenthal at the Permanent Subcommittee on Investigations.
  • An NBC exit poll found 56% of voters called AI or data centers a crucial or major factor, per TIME.
The money
  • Donors poured a record $12B into the midterms, including $446M from Elon Musk, yet Democrat James Talarico, whom Musk spent heavily to defeat, still won Texas's Senate seat, per Fortune's CEO Daily.
  • Pallone and Raskin's May letter sought Larry Ellison–Trump communications tied to the Paramount–WBD merger, and Warren's letter asked Amazon about its $40M Melania documentary.
Historical context
  • 2006 was the last midterm in which Democrats flipped both chambers at once, and Henry Waxman's House Oversight Committee then launched a wave of investigations.
Watch for
  • TIME expects hearings with AI executives and a possible push to undo efforts to preempt state AI laws, while a major federal AI law likely faces a Trump veto.
  • Ninth Circuit arguments on Nexstar–TEGNA on Nov. 17 and Skydance's first Q3 report on Nov. 9 will show how deal-makers price the new scrutiny.
