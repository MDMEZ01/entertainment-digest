Skydance's $80B Debt Meets Elon's Wallet

Why it matters
  • John Malone predicted at the Paley International Council Summit that SpaceX could invest roughly $8–10B in Skydance, sending shares up about 11% on Oct. 21.
  • Malone said a deal would "take a little bit of the burden off David Ellison's shoulders," a rare public read on the post-WBD balance sheet.
  • Puck's Bill Cohan reports Skydance stock near $7.75, well below the $12 price at which RedBird and Laurene Powell Jobs bought in.
The money
  • Skydance carries roughly $80B of debt after closing its $110B Warner Bros. Discovery acquisition on Oct. 6, per TipRanks and The Ankler.
  • RedBird put in $4B at $12 a share, and Powell Jobs' $250M stake is "already down a cool $90 million," Cohan writes.
Historical context
  • Warner Bros. Discovery itself spent years deleveraging after its 2022 merger left it with roughly $55B of debt, weighing on its stock and spending.
Watch for
  • Any filing or confirmation of SpaceX talks, and whether the $30M 'Clayface' signals a leaner DC film strategy under Ellison.
  • Whether Larry Ellison's pledged SpaceX and Skydance shares become part of the financing conversation.
