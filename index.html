📈 Today's Deep Dive

Netflix Reports Tonight With Its Content Leadership Gutted

Why it matters
  • Ted Sarandos fired content chief Bela Bajaria and then film chief Dan Lin, consolidating both jobs under ex-Disney TV boss Peter Rice, who starts Oct. 26.
  • Three sources tell Puck's Matt Belloni that Netflix is pursuing a deal with Fox One that could bring Sunday NFL games to the service; both companies declined to comment.
  • Nielsen puts Netflix at 7.8% of U.S. TV viewing versus YouTube's 14.2%, per The Ankler, which quotes one ex-Fox colleague calling Rice's hire "one of the most idiotic appointments."
The money
  • Consensus calls for about $12.87B in Q3 revenue and $0.82 EPS, and options imply an ~8.4% move off Monday's $69.85 close, near the 52-week low of $65.08.
  • Belloni notes engagement rose only 2% in the first half despite about $20B a year in content spend, as roughly 1,000 layoffs (about 6% of staff) hit last week.
Historical context
  • Puck says the stock's 40% one-year drop is its worst since 2022, the year Netflix's first subscriber loss in a decade forced the ad tier and the password-sharing crackdown.
Watch for
  • How management frames Rice's theatrical push (Narnia, the KPop Demon Hunters sequel) and any sports-rights expansion on tonight's call.
  • Who replaces Lin: Rice runs film in the interim, with Mike De Luca and Pam Abdy rumored and The Ankler also floating Emma Watts or Stacey Snider.
