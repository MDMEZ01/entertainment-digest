📈 Today's Deep Dive

Skydance Day One: Ellison's ~$110B Bet Opens Tuesday Under a Debt Cloud

Why it matters
  • The merged Paramount–WBD formally launches Oct. 6 as Skydance (SKYD), with Warner Bros. and Paramount as sub-brands.
  • Credit markets flinched first: Paramount bonds sold off and the cost of insuring its debt hit a 17-year high, Puck reports, citing Bloomberg.
  • PSKY closed down 9% on Oct. 1 and fell below $10, roughly half its value before the deal saga began, per The Wakeup.
The money
  • The $52 billion debt package left some bonds yielding over 10% (Barron's), while co-CEO Ynon Kreiz is tasked with delivering $6 billion in synergies.
Historical context
  • AT&T paid $85 billion for Time Warner in 2018 and spun it off within four years; the resulting Warner Bros. Discovery launched in 2022 with about $55 billion of debt.
Watch for
  • Whether Mark Thompson re-signs at CNN and where Bari Weiss lands.
  • Whether Bloys bundles HBO Max with Paramount+, and how many more jobs the $6 billion savings target costs.
