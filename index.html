📈 Today's Deep Dive

The Skydance Era Starts Tomorrow — With an $80 Billion Bill Attached

Why it matters
  • Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery close Tuesday as Skydance (SKYD), the third-largest studio behind Netflix and Disney.
  • The combined HBO Max and Paramount+ base is about 220M subscribers, and both brands will survive inside a bundle.
  • Ellison's slim set of direct reports — Kreiz, Gordon, Bloys, Greenstein/Goldberg, Cheeks, Weiss — now controls CBS, CNN, HBO, DC and two film studios.
The money
  • TheWrap puts the new company's debt at about $80 billion against a $6 billion cost-cutting target.
  • Investor Ross Gerber estimates every 1% rise in rates adds about $800M in costs, just as rates are climbing.
Historical context
  • The Ankler compares the WBTV–Paramount TV fusion to Disney after its 2019 Fox acquisition, an integration Disney took several years to complete.
Watch for
  • SKYD's first trading day Tuesday, after moving its listing from Nasdaq to the NYSE, and the priorities Ellison and Kreiz lay out, including the staff cuts the trades expect.
  • Who runs Warner Bros. Pictures under Greenstein and Goldberg, with a consent decree requiring 30 films a year.
