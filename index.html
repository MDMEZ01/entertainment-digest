📈 Today's Deep Dive

The Data-Center Backlash Arrives: Voters Just Repriced the AI Build-Out

Why it matters
  • Per Ballotpedia, all but one of 41 data-center ballot measures on Nov. 3 went against data centers, and 13 municipalities approved outright bans.
  • Texas governor-elect Gina Hinojosa vowed to pause new data centers until rules exist, while Microsoft passed on doubling an Nscale Texas contract and tightened oversight of Nebius and IREN projects.
  • Opposition went transatlantic Friday as protesters broke into Microsoft's Amsterdam site during 'Stop Dirty Data Centres' actions in 15 countries.
The money
  • Goldman Sachs economists estimate 45% of planned U.S. data-center expansion will be affected, and Nebius, IREN and CoreWeave have each lost about half their value in a month.
  • Builders are routing around hostile ground: Google committed $17B to a Wyoming campus, and OpenAI's new compact pledges to pay for its own energy.
Historical context
  • Long Island's Shoreham nuclear plant was finished in 1984 but never ran commercially; local and state opposition after Three Mile Island led to its 1989 decommissioning, the template for politics stranding built infrastructure.
Watch for
  • CoreWeave reports Nov. 10 and Nebius Nov. 12, the first read on whether neocloud demand is cracking.
  • Whether Anthropic can start its roadshow this week and still list in November.
