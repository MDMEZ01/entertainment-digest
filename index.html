Nvidia's Huang Crisis Turns Political

Why it matters
  • Nvidia hit $166.95 Tuesday, down 18.4% for November and wiping out its 2026 gains, with more than $470B erased in two sessions, per Decrypt.
  • Sen. Kevin Cramer called on Jensen Huang to resign over 'the potential for extortion,' while Trump said the FBI is 'looking into it' but that Huang should stay, per Forbes.
  • Eye on AI reports the woman at the center runs Megaspeed, alleged to have routed Nvidia chips to Chinese firms, and that the NYT says Nvidia's general counsel moved a Commerce probe of it to DOJ in 2025.
The money
  • Wedbush's Dan Ives says Nvidia is worth about 30% less without Huang, arguing 'Jensen is not replaceable,' per BI.
  • Baird's Tristan Gerra cut his target to $200 from $500 and downgraded to neutral, and Morgan Stanley dropped Nvidia as a top pick, though shares opened Wednesday up 2.5% at $172.68, per The National.
Historical context
  • Nvidia is on track for its worst month since September 2022, when it fell 19.6%, and sits 31.4% below its $243.37 peak, per Decrypt.
Watch for
  • Whether Huang met Rep. Brian Mast's Wednesday deadline to agree to a Nov. 18 closed-door briefing, or faces a subpoena, per ABC News.
  • The Wachtell Lipton review led by ex-SDNY chief Joon H. Kim, and Trump's APEC meeting with Xi in Shenzhen next week as export-control bills gain backers.
