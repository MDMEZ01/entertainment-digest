Nvidia's Board Turns Its Spotlight on Jensen Huang

Why it matters
  • Nvidia's board said Sunday it is investigating whether Huang “has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort,” with independent directors and outside counsel overseeing the probe.
  • Huang told staff “I was unfaithful to my wife” after NYT reporting that U.S. officials believe former Megaspeed executive Alice Huang was a Chinese intelligence asset.

  • Shares traded about 5% lower premarket Monday at $177.32 versus Friday's $186.64 close, a drop BI says would erase about $190B in value.

  • Intel CEO Brian Krzanich resigned in June 2018 after a board inquiry found a past consensual relationship with an employee violated company policy — but no national-security question hung over that case.

  • House China Committee members asked Huang to respond by Nov. 11 to a request for a Nov. 18 closed-door briefing.
  • Nvidia's Nov. 17 fiscal Q3 earnings call, the first time Huang faces analysts since the probe began.
