The Blue Wave Hits the AI Trade: Tech's Worst Day Since the 2025 Tariff Shock

Why it matters
  • The Nasdaq fell 5.52% on Nov. 5 to about 24,315, entering a correction, as the S&P 500 lost 4.36% and the Dow 3.44%.
  • Chips led the rout: the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 12.2%, with Nvidia down 9.3% and Micron down about 14%.
  • Wedbush's Dan Ives estimated at least 10% of planned U.S. AI data centers could come off the roadmap after voters backed restrictions.
The money
  • Tesla and SpaceX shed about $456B in two days, cutting Musk's fortune by roughly $113B (Fortune CEO Daily).
  • The selloff spread to Asia on Nov. 6, where TSMC hit limit-down and Anthropic's IPO investors now float about $1T instead of $1.5T.
Historical context
  • Fortune's CEO Daily called it the Nasdaq's steepest drop since the April 2025 tariff shock, when the index fell nearly 6% on back-to-back days (April 3–4).
Watch for
  • Whether Anthropic still starts its roadshow the week of Nov. 9, and how the market greets Skydance (Nov. 9) and Disney (Nov. 17) earnings.
  • More pay-our-own-power pledges like Google's $17B Wyoming site, as builders try to win back local voters.
