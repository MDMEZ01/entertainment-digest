Nvidia's $900 Billion Week: The AI Trade Meets an Accounting Probe

Why it matters
  • Nvidia closed Friday at $125.53, down 14.01%, after reports the SEC probe is examining its accounting, its worst day since the January 2025 DeepSeek rout.
  • It delayed its 10-Q, citing an investigation by an independent board committee, and slipped to fourth in U.S. market value behind Apple, Microsoft and Alphabet.
  • The contagion was broad: the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 10.73% with all 30 stocks lower, while CoreWeave dropped 12.1% and Nebius 16.1%.
The money
  • About $490B in value vanished Friday and nearly $900B for the week, with roughly $2.7T lost since the Oct. 6 peak.
  • Jensen Huang lost about $17.3B on Friday alone, leaving him near $110.2B, per Forbes.
Historical context
  • Yahoo Finance reports the week was Nvidia's worst since October 2008, when the financial crisis hit tech stocks.
Watch for
  • Treasury Secretary Bessent wants "answers in the next 10 days from the board and from the outside counsel."
  • California AG Rob Bonta subpoenaed Nvidia on Nov. 19 over ex-CEO Huang's alleged China ties, and Huang is due to testify to House committees Dec. 1.
