📈 Today's Deep Dive

Netflix's Reset: The Chart, the Cuts and the Pulled Xbox Deal

Why it matters
  • Netflix's share of Nielsen's U.S. originals top-10 fell from 80% to 51% this year, and from 76% to 36% across all charts, per Entertainment Strategy Guy.
  • That slide frames the firing of Bela Bajaria and Dan Lin and about 1,000 layoffs, with Peter Rice starting as chief content officer next week.
  • Belloni argues the programming strategy may not change much, with Rice focused on U.S. scripted TV and better films.
The money
  • Bajaria had offered $500M over seven years for exclusive Xbox film/TV rights; Xbox countered at $800M, and Netflix pulled the offer after her exit.
  • Netflix is still growing about 12% a year with a 34.6% operating margin, per Puck, while Prime Video's chart share rose from 5% to 15%.
Historical context
  • Netflix last reset this sharply after losing 200,000 subscribers in Q1 2022, its first loss in over a decade, which led to its ad tier and password-sharing crackdown.
Watch for
  • Who replaces Lin as film chief; Mike De Luca and Pam Abdy are talking to Rice, while Disney's David Greenbaum and Sean Bailey say they are not.
  • Whether Rice chases one-off NFL games and other sports rights, as Puck reports he wants.
