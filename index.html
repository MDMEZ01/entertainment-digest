📈 Today's Deep Dive

Anthropic's S-1 Shows an AI Lab That Makes Money on Compute — and Owes Hundreds of Billions for It

Why it matters
  • Anthropic says it earned more than $2 of revenue for every $1 of compute by June, with a 19% adjusted operating margin last quarter.
  • First-half 2026 revenue hit $16.3B, but the net loss was $80.7B, and the S-1 warns margins "may compress" as it scales.
  • Trading, under the Nasdaq ticker ANT, is expected sometime between Nov. 9 and Thanksgiving, per Fortune's Eye on AI.
The money
  • Fortune tallies $542B–$567B in committed compute and infrastructure spending, including up to $120.5B with SpaceX through 2029.
  • Compute was the largest operating expense at $5.6B in Q2, against $11.6B of Q2 revenue.
Historical context
  • Like Google in 2004 and Facebook in 2012, Anthropic is going public with founder control locked in: its seven cofounders hold 50.1% of the vote through a Founders LLC, and a Long-Term Benefit Trust appoints a board majority.
Watch for
  • Whether Q3 numbers briefed at the Oct. 14 investor day, but left out of the S-1, surface before pricing.
  • How Michael Burry's bearish take and retail jitters, flagged by Business Insider, shape day-one demand.
