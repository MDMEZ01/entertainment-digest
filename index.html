Today's Deep Dive

Netflix Buys a Hollywood Lot at 20 Cents on the Dollar

Why it matters
  • Netflix bought the 55-acre, 22-stage Radford Studio Center for $380M, about 20% of the $1.85B Hackman Capital paid in 2021.
  • The world's largest streamer is putting down roots in Los Angeles as production keeps leaving the city; its memo said, 'This city is home to us.'
  • Netflix plans to consolidate production now spread across its leased Sunset and Raleigh studio space onto the lot.
The money
  • Goldman Sachs-led lenders took the lot back after Hackman defaulted and sold it directly to Netflix rather than at auction.
  • Netflix also paid the city's 5.5% 'mansion tax' on the sale, which puts the all-in cost near $400M.
Historical context
  • Netflix's first studio purchase was ABQ Studios in New Mexico in 2018; Radford, opened in 1928, gives it a historic lot in its home market.
Watch for
  • What happens to Netflix's Sunset leases, which run to 2031, as work moves to Radford.
  • Whether other distressed L.A. studio assets trade at similar discounts while Congress weighs a federal production credit.
