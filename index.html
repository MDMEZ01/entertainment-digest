📈 Today's Deep Dive

Anthropic Pays Up for Compute as IPO Investors Push Back on Price

Why it matters
  • Anthropic offered Blackstone a 10%+ yield to take $9B of an $18B junior tranche in a $60B "Project North Star" financing for about 1.5 GW of Google TPUs.
  • That premium compares with about 7% on comparable BB-rated bonds and 8.5% on Anthropic's "Project Big Sky" four months ago, so lenders are charging more to fund AI compute.
  • At a San Francisco investor day this week, some IPO investors pushed the valuation toward $1.5 trillion from about $2 trillion.
The money
  • Q3 revenue reached $16.8B, up 45% from $11.6B in Q2, with an adjusted profit.
  • Compute commitments total at least $518B over a decade, and the TPU debt is backed by five-year leases.
Historical context
  • Uber is the cautionary template: bankers floated valuations up to $120B in 2018, but its May 2019 IPO valued the company at about $82B.
Watch for
  • Final IPO pricing in November, and whether Anthropic accepts the lower range.
  • Anthropic's talks with the NYC Comptroller, which sent a letter urging a delay, plus Friday's tech sell-off and 10-year yields above 5%.
