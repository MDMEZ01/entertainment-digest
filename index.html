📈 Today's Deep Dive

WarnerMount's Power Map Takes Shape Before the Deal Even Closes

Why it matters
  • Cindy Holland's Tuesday exit makes HBO's Casey Bloys the presumptive programmer of a combined HBO Max–Paramount+ with 200M+ subscribers.
  • Puck reports Warner insiders calling the Ellison era "like doomsday," bracing for "thousands and thousands of layoffs" done quickly.
  • Ynon Kreiz, just out at Mattel, is nearing a senior role at the combined company per THR and Bloomberg, signaling Ellison will import outside operators.
The money
  • The $110B deal is backed by a $44B bond sale, and the settlement requires $1.5B in U.S. production over five years plus 30 films a year.
  • Missing the film quota could force Paramount to sell its 49% Miramax stake, per Puck, while a roughly $7M-a-day ticking fee starts Thursday, per The Wakeup.
Historical context
  • Disney's $71.3B Fox takeover closed in March 2019 and was followed within weeks by thousands of layoffs as Fox executive ranks were folded in.
Watch for
  • Judge Martínez-Olguín's ruling on the 12-state settlement, which gates the early-October close.
  • Fates of CFO Gunnar Wiedenfels and film chiefs Mike De Luca and Pam Abdy, which Puck flags as open questions.
