📈 Today's Deep Dive

Skydance Is Official: Hollywood's Biggest Studio Now Runs on Debt Math

Why it matters
  • Paramount closed its Warner Bros. Discovery takeover on Oct. 6, paying $31.02 a share in cash; the combined company trades as SKYD on the NYSE.
  • The roughly $110B deal unites Warner Bros., Paramount, HBO, CBS and CNN under CEO David Ellison and co-CEO Ynon Kreiz.
  • The closing came after Justice Elena Kagan denied a last-minute bid to halt it.
The money
  • Puck cites about $79B in net debt by Skydance's own numbers, with recent rate moves adding roughly $400M a year in interest.
  • Skydance promises $6B in run-rate synergies within three years, $10B+ in free cash flow by 2030 and $30B+ in annual content spend.
Historical context
  • The Ankler compares it to Discovery's April 2022 takeover of WarnerMedia, and notes that after Disney bought Fox, four TV studios became one within a few years.
Watch for
  • Where the cuts land: a state settlement fines Skydance $30M for each film short of 30 releases a year, so back-office jobs and duplicate TV studios are the likelier targets.
  • Needham's Laura Martin warns 'the melting iceberg of linear TV' may outrun streaming growth; analysts don't expect streaming traction until 2030.
