📈 Today's Deep Dive

Skydance's Debt Is Already Trading Like a Warning Sign

Why it matters
  • The $110B WBD deal left about $82B of net debt, nearly 7x a projected ~$12B of 2026 EBITDA before $6B of hoped-for synergies.
  • Bank of America priced the bonds aggressively, and they fell right away: the $5.2B 10-year trades near 97 cents and the 8.875% eight-year junk bond yields 10%.
  • Millennium bought $2.2B of the new paper and dumped it, losing as much as $100M, Puck's Bill Cohan reports.
The money
  • Larry Ellison put in about $17B more (1.4B shares at $12) on top of $6B earlier, roughly 15% of his ~$185B fortune; Gulf sovereign funds added $24B and RedBird $4B (RedBird also invests in Puck).
  • Fitch says the Ellisons pledged to cut leverage to 3.75x by FY2028 and 3x by 2029, which would take "equity issuance or asset sales."
Historical context
  • WBD itself launched in 2022 carrying about $55B of debt from AT&T's WarnerMedia spin and spent four years paying it down; Skydance starts the next cycle far deeper in the hole.
Watch for
  • Whether Skydance sells assets or issues equity to hit Fitch's targets, and whether bond spreads keep widening from ~150bp over investment grade.
  • Integration layoffs, which the co-CEOs were pressed on at their town hall, plus write-downs like "Digger" hitting the Q4 books.
