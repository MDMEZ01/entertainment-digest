📈 Today's Deep Dive

Skydance's 22% Plunge Shows Wall Street Doubts the WBD Debt Load

Why it matters
  • Skydance shares closed down 22.2% at $7.06 on Oct. 14 after the company distributed about 470.7M warrants to Class B holders.
  • Puck's Dylan Byers called it a sign of Wall Street's dissatisfaction with the leverage ratio, putting the market cap at "just $36 billion."
  • Deadline framed the drop as a technical adjustment, but it came the same day hot CPI data sent the Nasdaq down 1.88%.
The money
  • Each warrant can buy a share at $12 until October 2036, so full exercise would raise about $5.7B.
  • The combined company carries about $80B of debt and targets about $6B of savings, and its new bonds trailed JPMorgan's high-grade index by 27 basis points over 10 days.
Historical context
  • Warner Bros. Discovery debuted in April 2022 carrying roughly $55B of debt and lost more than half its market value in its first year as deleveraging dominated the story.
Watch for
  • Netflix's Oct. 20 earnings, its first report since losing WBD to Skydance, as a read on how investors price the rival models.
  • Whether Skydance speeds up combining HBO Max and Paramount+ to show savings faster.
