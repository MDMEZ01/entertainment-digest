Nvidia as Co-Signer: The AI Build-Out Turns to Vendor Financing

Why it matters
  • Nvidia is weighing a residual value guarantee on billions of Nscale debt for Anthropic's West Virginia data center, putting its balance sheet behind a customer's customer.
  • It comes as neocloud stocks CoreWeave, Nebius and IREN each fell more than 30% in October, a sign lenders and investors want a backstop.
  • S&P cut Meta's outlook to negative Oct. 30 over AI capex, so even the strongest buyers now face credit pressure.
The money
  • Anthropic's contract for the first 460MW Vera Rubin building is $44.6B, with talks on a second building worth about $45B.
  • Nvidia already has about $170B in investments and credit support extended to neoclouds and large customers.
Historical context
  • In the 1999–2000 telecom boom, Lucent and Nortel lent billions to carriers to buy their gear, and the losses hit when those customers failed.
Watch for
  • Whether the guarantee is finalized and how Anthropic's IPO roadshow, possibly starting the week of Nov. 9, presents these compute commitments.
  • More AI bond deals after Microsoft's $25B sale, its first since 2017.
