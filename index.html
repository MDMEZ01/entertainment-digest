📈 Today's Deep Dive

The $1.5 Trillion Debut: Anthropic Prices the Biggest IPO Ever

Why it matters
  • Anthropic priced at $83 a share Wednesday night, above its $70–$78 range, and begins trading on Nasdaq today as ANT — the market's first big pure-play bet on a frontier AI lab.
  • Demand let it upsize to 980M shares (from 825M), raising about $81.3B at a ~$1.5T fully diluted valuation, up from $965B in its May private round.
  • Pre-listing indications on Hyperliquid (~$102.50) and Lang & Schwarz (up to $107) pointed to a first-day pop of roughly 20–26%.
The money
  • AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC Partners also bought $26B of non-voting shares at the IPO price; Amazon owns nearly 15% and Google almost 14%.
  • The amended S-1 shows Q3 revenue of about $17B (from $11.6B in Q2), a $567M operating loss, ~$132B in cash and ~$895B in compute and other commitments.
Historical context
  • At $81.3B it tops SpaceX's roughly $75B June listing and nearly triples Saudi Aramco's 2019 record of $29.4B including its greenshoe.
Watch for
  • Today's opening print, and whether underwriters exercise their option on up to 147M more shares.
  • Economist Owen Lamont now puts AI-bubble odds at 70% and told Fortune a strong Anthropic debut 'would add to the evidence.'
