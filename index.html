📈 Today’s Deep Dive

Why Paramount May Want to Lose the WBD Trial — Fast

Why It Matters
  • Paramount froze its $111B Warner Bros. Discovery merger until a ruling or June 2027, handing the 12-state coalition the functional equivalent of the injunction it sought — yet both sides claim victory.
  • Calif. AG Rob Bonta told The Ankler he wants the deal blocked outright and is “not talking settlement,” casting it as a stand against “oligarchs or kings in the economy.”
The Money
  • Starting Oct. 1, Paramount owes WBD shareholders ~$650M per quarter — about $6.9M a day — for every quarter the deal stays unclosed.
  • That’s nearly $2B if the case drags to June 2027, which is exactly why a fast trial — even a quick loss that speeds an appeal — may beat bleeding cash indefinitely.
Historical Context
  • The judge rejected Paramount’s argument that Netflix and Apple already make the market competitive — a warning that “Big Tech is the real rival” defenses face skepticism in media antitrust.
Watch For
  • July 31: a joint scheduling proposal is due, setting the trial timetable.
  • U.K. pressure is building — stars Cumberbatch, Cumming and Wong urged Britain to block the deal as culture secretary Lisa Nandy weighs intervening.
