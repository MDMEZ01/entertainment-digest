📈 Today's Deep Dive

Fox Closes Its $22B Roku Deal: Murdoch's Network Now Owns the TV Front Door

Why it matters
  • Roku now sits inside Fox Digital Platforms alongside Tubi, Fox One and Red Seat Ventures, run by co-chairs Paul Cheesbrough and Roku founder Anthony Wood.
  • Roku's connected-TV platform reaches more than 100 million households, giving Fox the operating system that every rival streamer depends on.
  • Mustafa Ozgen stays Roku CEO, while Mike Bidgoli succeeds the departing Anjali Sud as Tubi president.
The money
  • Roku holders got $95.30 in cash plus 0.9799 Fox Class A shares per share, leaving them about 26% of the combined company.
  • Fox expects about $400 million in annualized cost savings within two years.
Historical context
  • Fox bought Tubi for $440 million in 2020; it now pays roughly 50 times that to own the platform distributing Tubi and its competitors.
Watch for
  • Whether Lachlan Murdoch's pledge to keep Roku's open distribution model a priority holds when Netflix, Disney and Amazon negotiate carriage and ad terms.
  • How quickly Fox folds Roku's ad inventory into Tubi and Fox One after closing less than six months after announcing the deal.
