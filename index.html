📈 Today's Deep Dive

Anthropic Opens the Book on a ~$90B Listing

Why it matters
  • Anthropic set a $70–$78 range for 825M shares and launched its roadshow, with trading on Nasdaq as ANT expected to begin Nov. 19, per CNBC.
  • Valuation reads diverge: The Decoder puts it up to about $1.02T, while ABC7 reports about $1.3T, likely on a fully diluted basis.
  • It lands the same week ChatGPT holds more than 81% of traffic to the six major chatbots, per Graphite via BI.
The money
  • Up to $64.4B would be raised publicly plus a $26B private placement, about $90.4B in all, with Goldman Sachs lead-left among 22 banks.
  • JPMorgan leads domestic retail, and Anthropic has weighed a 15–20% retail tranche.
Historical context
  • For scale, Alibaba raised $25B in its 2014 IPO and Saudi Aramco $29.4B in 2019; Anthropic's public tranche alone would be more than double Aramco's.
Watch for
  • A livestreamed IPO conversation between Dario Amodei and JPMorgan's Jamie Dimon, expected Friday.
  • Whether pricing lands at the top of the range, after OpenAI's $11B close at a $1.25T valuation Nov. 6–7 came in below its ~$1.4T target.
