📈 Today's Deep Dive

Nvidia Loses Its Founder: Huang Resigns, Kress Steps In

Why it matters
  • Nvidia's board accepted Jensen Huang's resignation on Nov. 11 and named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO while she keeps the CFO role.
  • Director Harvey C. Jones, on the board since 1993, became Nvidia's first-ever board chair as the company searches for a permanent CEO.
  • The exit followed a NYT report of a second affair, with VP of healthcare Kimberly Powell, after reports tied Huang to Megaspeed executive Alice Huang, whom U.S. officials believed was passing information to Beijing.
The money
  • Nvidia shed more than $600B in market value this week, and CNBC reported the stock falling 12% Thursday as the resignation was announced.
  • Dan Ives had said a Huang exit would shave 30% off the stock; Huang owns about 3.6% of the company.
Historical context
  • Intel's Brian Krzanich resigned in 2018 over a relationship with an employee, and Uber's board forced out founder Travis Kalanick in 2017; Fortune's Diane Brady cites the Apple-after-Jobs and Uber-after-Kalanick precedents.
Watch for
  • House GOP chairs requested a closed-door Nvidia briefing on Nov. 18, and Trump said the FBI is looking into the matter.
  • Anthropic's IPO, in which Nvidia joins the $26B private placement, prices next week in the turmoil's shadow.
