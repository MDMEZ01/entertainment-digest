Nvidia Without Huang: A Spy Scandal Becomes a Policy Reckoning

Why it matters
  • Jensen Huang quit as CEO and director after a board probe into his relationship with a woman U.S. officials believe was recruited by China's Ministry of State Security.
  • Puck reports the alleged spy is a former Megaspeed executive, tying the scandal to existing questions over whether Nvidia's Asian chip sales broke export controls.
  • With Democrats about to control Congress, Puck says the question is shifting to "what the White House knew, and when."
The money
  • Nvidia lost nearly $700B in market value this week, per Puck, closing Friday at $162.85 after a roughly 12% weekly drop.
  • HSBC cut the stock to Hold and slashed its target to $170 from $365, while 24/7 Wall St. flags $36B in off-balance-sheet backstops for neoclouds.
Historical context
  • Intel's Brian Krzanich resigned in June 2018 over a past relationship with an employee, and Intel ran on an interim CEO for seven months before naming Bob Swan.
Watch for
  • Interim CEO Colette Kress leads Tuesday's Nov. 17 earnings call, against guidance of $108.0B after last quarter's $96.2B.
  • Three House committees want a closed-door Huang interview Dec. 1 on threat of subpoena, and Sen. Tim Scott wants Huang and Commerce Secretary Lutnick to testify in December (AFP).
