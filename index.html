📈 Today's Deep Dive

Warner Bros.' Last Week: Ellison Redraws the Studio Map Before Tuesday's Close

Why it matters
  • Mike De Luca and Pam Abdy will not join the merged company and will be paid out through 2030, per Puck; Paramount's Josh Greenstein and Dana Goldberg will oversee both studios and the promised 30 releases a year.
  • Casey Bloys is set to program the combined streamers, George Cheeks takes a bigger job including TV studios, and Mark Thompson stays at CNN — announcements expected early next week.
  • Belloni writes that "as of next week, the century-old Warner Bros. studio will cease to exist" as a stand-alone company.
The money
  • Belloni calls it a "$110 billion deal"; Paramount's studio generated more than $200M of adjusted EBITDA in the first half of 2026, while Warners' 2026 slate has yet to produce a $100M domestic grosser.
  • David Zaslav can leave with "up to $800 million" in bonuses and stock grants, per Puck.
Historical context
  • Disney's 2019 $71.3B purchase of Fox's entertainment assets folded 20th Century Fox into Disney, which dropped "Fox" from the studio's name in 2020 — a reminder that legacy studio brands rarely survive absorption intact.
Watch for
  • The deal's expected Tuesday (Oct. 6) close, the outside or internal hire to run Warners, and whether Peter Safran and James Gunn stay at DC beyond the initial invitation.
  • Ellison and Ynon Kreiz are expected to ring the NYSE bell next Thursday.

11 Million: U.S. Workers Who May Need New Careers by 2035

The figure
  • About 11M U.S. workers (~6.5% of the labor force) may need to switch occupations entirely by 2035 due to automation and AI — McKinsey Global Institute, released Sept. 29, 2026.
Context
  • Automation could cut labor demand by 36M jobs while growth creates demand for 40M.
  • About 25M affected workers can stay in their current occupations.
  • McKinsey: "The next decade's challenge is mobility, not scarcity."
So what
  • For entertainment and media, the bottleneck is retraining crews and staff — not a shortage of work.
