Skydance's Sports Triage Is the First Real Test of Its $80B Bet

Why it matters
  • CBS Sports president David Berson now chairs the combined group, with the NFL, March Madness, the Masters and UFC deemed untouchable (Puck).
  • TNT's College Football Playoff, Big 12 and French Open deals could be dropped, Barrett Media reports, as smaller Silberwasser-era deals look 'vulnerable.'
  • Skydance will favor sports that drive both affiliate fees and Paramount+ subscriptions, John Ourand reports; one executive expects 'a bloodbath.'
The money
  • Skydance carries about $80B of debt against a $6B synergy target set when the $111B deal closed Oct. 6.
  • TNT's MLB and NHL packages, worth over $700M a year and expiring in 2028, are under scrutiny.
Historical context
  • Warner Bros. Discovery already lost the NBA when the league's 11-year deals went to ESPN, NBC and Amazon starting in 2025–26.
Watch for
  • A final sports structure within 'a month or two,' plus an NFL renegotiation and a possible run at international soccer rights.
  • MLB's proposed exclusive Monday-night window, a new package once national deals expire after 2028.
