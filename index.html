The Paramount-WBD Merger's Next Battleground: Austin vs. Los Angeles

Why It Matters
  • LA County's own economic development department — not an outside advocacy group — is now the source putting a hard number on the merger's damage: 10,360 job-years, a $2.78B economic hit, $1.26B in lost wages.
  • Mayor Bass's shift from conditional opposition to urging a "quick settlement" is a political tell: facing reelection and her own economists' warnings, she's betting resolution beats stalemate — a break from mayoral candidate Nithya Raman, who says she "stand[s] with the workers fighting this merger," and drawing a WGA rebuke that Paramount "created a false choice for entertainment industry workers." New Today
  • Ellison scouting a 415,000-sq-ft Austin site — bigger than Paramount's current LA lot — turns the relocation threat from legal rhetoric into a real address.
The Money
  • The county's report tallies costs largely in property-tax losses, meaning the fiscal hit shows up in city and county budgets before any single job is formally cut.
  • Ellison has set a hard internal deadline: begin exiting California by Oct. 1 if no settlement is reached by Sept. 30, with Georgia and Tennessee also under consideration as alternates to Austin.
Historical Context
  • The Austin scouting echoes Tesla's 2021 headquarters move to Texas amid California regulatory friction — a playbook of using relocation as leverage that California has struggled to counter.
Watch For
  • Whether Bonta responds to Bass's and Cinema United's settlement push, or holds the line toward the March 2027 trial.
  • Sept. 24, when a judge rules on Paramount Skydance's $1.9B bond request — the next hard deadline before Ellison's self-imposed Oct. 1 exit trigger.
  • A separate but related Disney flashpoint: a judge just set the week of Oct. 5 for a hearing on Disney's First Amendment suit against the FCC over its ABC station-license review. New Today
