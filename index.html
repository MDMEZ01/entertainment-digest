The AI Unwind Becomes a Growth Scare

Why it matters
  • Goldman's AI basket is down 32% since Oct. 6, and the firm says the sell-off has widened from an AI unwind into a broader growth downgrade.
  • The S&P 500 fell 3.7% Friday to 6,910.39, its seventh straight loss, while the Nasdaq Composite had its worst week since March 2020.
  • Media stocks are caught in it: Skydance closed the week at $6.20 (down 53% this year) and Netflix at $56.73, per The Wakeup.
The money
  • Nvidia lost about $1.04 trillion in market value between its Nov. 11 close and its Nov. 20 intraday low, closing Friday down 14% at $125.53.
  • There is no flight to safety: the 10-year Treasury yield sits near 5%, and foreign Treasury purchases fell 60% in the first eight months of 2026, per Fortune's CEO Daily.
Historical context
  • REX Financial's Bill Birmingham warns of a 'telecom style bust' tied to vendor financing, recalling the 2000–02 crash when equipment makers that lent to their own customers were hit hardest.
Watch for
  • Monday's bounce: S&P futures were up about 2.5% and Nvidia up 9% premarket; Goldman's bottom signals are AI-earnings confidence, a dovish Fed or a positioning washout.
  • October PCE inflation lands Wednesday, with markets pricing roughly 96–98% odds of a Dec. 9 Fed rate hike.
