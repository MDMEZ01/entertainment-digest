📈 Today's Deep Dive

The Price of WarnerMount: What Paramount Promised to Get Warner Bros.

Why it matters
  • The consent decree binds the merged studio to at least 30 theatrical releases a year for two years and 32 for the next three, each with a 45-day window.
  • Missing the quota costs $30M per film toward WGA health and pension funds and can force a sale of Miramax.
  • A five-member board of veteran journalists, due within 180 days of close, will monitor CBS News and CNN.
The money
  • The deal is worth $81B in equity and about $111B including debt, with an Oct. 6 close expected.
  • Puck says new co-CEO Ynon Kreiz must find $6B in synergies, even as the decree adds $1.5B of U.S. production spending over five years.
Historical context
  • Comcast's 2011 NBCUniversal takeover likewise cleared only after regulators imposed a multi-year package of behavioral conditions.
Watch for
  • Day-one cuts once Kreiz takes over integration after the Oct. 6 close.
  • Entertainment Strategy Guy flags the deal's debt load as the "ugly side" that will shape every later decision.
