Today's Deep Dive

A Federal Judge Just Took the FCC's License Threat Off the Table for ABC

Why it matters
  • Judge Loren AliKhan on Nov. 25 barred the FCC from further action in its early license-renewal review of eight ABC-owned stations.
  • She found ABC likely to prove retaliation, writing 'This is not a close case' and citing Trump's repeated calls to revoke ABC's licenses.
  • The ruling blocks a hearing designation order, the procedural step that can lead toward license revocation.
The money
  • Owned-and-operated stations are Disney's direct broadcast footprint, and the injunction removes the near-term risk to those licenses.
  • The FCC's separate probes of Disney's employment practices and 'The View' are not covered and continue.
Historical context
  • The closest parallel is 1973, when Nixon allies filed challenges to the Washington Post Company's Florida TV licenses during Watergate coverage.
Watch for
  • The FCC's compliance report is due Dec. 2 and a joint report on next steps is due Dec. 9.
  • The FCC called the ruling 'wrong on the facts and wrong on the law' but has not said whether it will appeal.
