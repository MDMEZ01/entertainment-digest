Anthropic's S-1 Shows the Bill Behind a Historic IPO

Why it matters
  • Anthropic's amended S-1 shows Q3 revenue of $17B, up 47% quarter over quarter, slowing from Q2's 144% jump to $11.5B.
  • Q3 brought a $23.3B net loss, $21.9B of it accounting adjustments, plus a $567M operating loss after Q2's nearly $1B operating profit.
  • The talent race isn't waiting: Claude Code product lead Cat Wu left Oct. 30 to run core product management at OpenAI.
The money
  • Purchase commitments total "just shy of $900 billion," with $70B due within a year and $447.5B before the end of 2030.
  • Anthropic is seeking a $1.5T–$2T valuation, and the FT reports it may not get an investment-grade credit rating.
Historical context
  • Saudi Aramco's 2019 debut raised a record $25.6B at about a $1.7T valuation, the scale Anthropic's target now approaches.
Watch for
  • Bloomberg reported marketing could begin the week of Nov. 9.
  • Whether a Democratic Congress turns the FTC's AI-safety probe into hearings before pricing.
