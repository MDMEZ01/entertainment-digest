📈 Today's Deep Dive

Disney's First D'Amaro Year Closes With a Beat — and $3B in Cuts

Why it matters
  • Disney beat fiscal Q4 forecasts with adjusted EPS of $1.77 vs. $1.67 expected on revenue up 16% to $25.9B, yet shares fell nearly 2% premarket.
  • It pairs a $3B operating-expense cut target through FY2029 with a 9% rise in content spending, so the cuts fall on overhead, not programming.
  • Disney will stop disclosing streaming operating income, removing the metric investors used to judge its direct-to-consumer turnaround.
The money
  • Entertainment operating income rose 92% to $1.3B and Sports rose 53% to about $1.4B, while Experiences grew 20% to about $2.3B.
  • FY27 guidance calls for $26B in content spend, double-digit EPS growth, about $9B in buybacks and a dividend raised 33% to $2.
Historical context
  • In 2023 Bob Iger launched a $5.5B cost program with about 7,000 layoffs and later raised the target to $7.5B; this is the second big efficiency push in three years.
Watch for
  • Where the $3B in cuts land, since the layoffs flagged in recent weeks now have a dollar target.
  • Whether investors accept less streaming disclosure in exchange for buybacks and a higher dividend.
