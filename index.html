📈 Today's Deep Dive

Skydance's First Report Card: Wall Street Grades the Debt, Not the Dream

Why it matters
  • Fitch cut Skydance from BB+ to BB on day one, citing "materially higher leverage, significant execution and integration risks," while Moody's rates it Ba3.
  • Fitch puts net debt near 7x adjusted earnings through 2026–27, against management's target of 3x or less by 2029.
  • The stock fell on its first day of trading even as Ellison promised to outspend Disney and Netflix on content.
The money
  • Roughly $80B of debt sits against a $30B–$40B annual content budget, $6B+ of targeted run-rate synergies over three years and a goal of $10B+ free cash flow by 2030.
  • RedBird has put $4B of new equity into the company as part of a $6B total commitment, cementing its control of voting stock alongside the Ellisons.
Historical context
  • AT&T bought Time Warner for $85B in 2018 and spun WarnerMedia off to Discovery in 2022, launching Warner Bros. Discovery with a debt load that shaped every Zaslav-era cut until this sale.
Watch for
  • The size and timing of layoffs tied to the $6B savings target, and whether "non-labor" savings materialize.
  • Whether Moody's or S&P follow Fitch, and when the HBO Max–Paramount+ bundle launches as the first revenue test.
