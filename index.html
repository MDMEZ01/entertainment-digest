Has AI Capex Peaked? The Week That Tested the Trade

Why it matters
  • Thursday's rout cut the Nasdaq 2.6% to 26,008.86 and the SOX 6.72%, its worst day since June, with AMD down 8.1% and Nvidia 4.4%.
  • Tesla quietly cut 2026 capex guidance to "in excess of $20B" from "more than $25B," feeding fears that spending has topped out.
  • Goldman data showed all three major hedge-fund strategies lost more than 1% on Oct. 22, a first since the pandemic, with tech funds down about 10% in October.
The money
  • Demand signals still point the other way: AWS chief Andy Jassy said 2026 capacity is "sold out," 2027 "largely sold out" and "a good chunk of '28" spoken for.
  • Meta closed Thursday at $648.85, down 6.98%, after guiding capex higher while free cash flow turned negative.
Historical context
  • The 2000–02 telecom crash, when carriers' fiber buildout outran demand, is the standard precedent for an infrastructure boom reversing on investors.
Watch for
  • Microsoft, Alphabet and Amazon earnings next week, plus the Fed's Oct. 27–28 meeting, will show whether capex guidance holds.
  • Intel reports Oct. 29 after the close, and the Nasdaq enters the week after two straight weekly losses.
