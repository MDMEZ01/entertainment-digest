Why it matters
  • Orders reached nearly 7x the shares on offer per Bloomberg, drawing more demand than SpaceX's record listing.
  • BI Today calls it 'the cleanest bellwether for AI' and the first major test of appetite for a company focused solely on AI.

  • The $70–$78 range implies about $1.4T, versus a $965B private valuation in May.

Historical context
  • SpaceX priced at $135 in June and fell below that within a month, while Cerebras priced at $185 and now trades near $104.

  • Whether bankers lift the range, after the FT reported Nov. 10 they were weighing roughly $1.5T+.
