The $2.36 Billion Day: GTA VI Rewrites the Entertainment Launch

Why it matters
  • Sensor Tower estimates 'Grand Theft Auto VI' sold about 24.6M copies and made about $2.36B on its Nov. 19 launch day, roughly $2B of it from pre-orders.
  • About 75% of first-day copies (about 18.4M) were on PS5 and about 6.15M on Xbox, making it one of the biggest single-day launches in entertainment history.
  • The launch lands the same week as the 'Hunger Games' prequel's $21.25M previews, as the Entertainment Strategy Guy flags worries it could pull time from theaters and linear TV.
The money
  • Take-Two shares rose more than 6% to close at $216.61 on the eve of launch, with the game scoring 99 on Metacritic.
  • Rockstar and Take-Two have not confirmed any sales figures, so the Sensor Tower numbers remain estimates.
Historical context
  • GTA V sold about 11.2M copies and roughly $800M on its first day in September 2013, then crossed $1 billion in three days.
Watch for
  • Take-Two's first official sales number, and whether Rockstar's openness to a 'Red Dead Redemption' adaptation turns into a $100M+ Hollywood deal.
  • This weekend's box office and TV ratings, for any measurable GTA drag on 'Sunrise on the Reaping.'
