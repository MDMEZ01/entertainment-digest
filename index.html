📈 Today's Deep Dive

Netflix Fires Its Content Chief and Hands Peter Rice a $20B Reset

Why it matters
  • Ted Sarandos told Bela Bajaria on Monday she was out, the Ankler reports, and ex-Disney TV chairman Peter Rice takes over global content on Oct. 26.
  • Her exit followed 1,000 layoffs (about 6% of staff), and Puck says film chief Dan Lin is expected to follow, at Rice's discretion.
  • Netflix is courting Disney, Fox and NBCUniversal to run cable-like "channels" on its platform, per the Ankler.
The money
  • Rice inherits a roughly $20B content budget at a company whose stock is down about 40% since last year's failed Warner Bros. bid.
  • Engagement grew just 2% in the first half, and Netflix had 7.8% of July connected-TV viewing vs. YouTube's 14.2% (Nielsen, via Puck).
Historical context
  • Rice ran Fox Searchlight and managed the Fox-to-Disney integration before Bob Chapek fired him in 2022, experience Puck says could matter if Netflix pursues a studio such as a spun-off NBCUniversal.
Watch for
  • Q3 earnings on Tuesday, Oct. 20, which one insider told the Ankler is "probably going to be the worst quarter in the last five years."
  • Whether Rice replaces Lin and how Netflix explains the reset to Wall Street.
