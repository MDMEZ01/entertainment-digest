Netflix Bets on a Studio Integrator Before a High-Stakes Earnings Call

Why it matters
  • Netflix replaced content chief Bela Bajaria with Peter Rice on Oct. 15, handing him a content budget reported at about $20B.
  • Ted Sarandos called Rice "one of the most effective at-scale operators in the entertainment industry," and film chief Dan Lin's position is now in question.
  • Puck notes Rice's Fox–Disney merger experience "could be valuable to Netflix if it tries to buy another studio. Like, say, NBCUniversal."
The money
  • Netflix guided Q3 to $12.86B in revenue (about +12%), a 33.2% operating margin and $0.82 EPS, with results due Tuesday, Oct. 20.
  • Shares closed at $70.57 on Oct. 14, down about 42% in a year, after roughly 1,000 layoffs, its biggest cut since 2022.
Historical context
  • Rice ran the integration of 21st Century Fox into Disney after the $71.3B deal closed in 2019, and was fired by Bob Chapek in 2022.
Watch for
  • Whether Tuesday's call addresses the "channels" strategy The Ankler says is coming, plus theatrical plans like 45-day windows for "La Bola Negra" and Gerwig's "Narnia."
  • Any word on Dan Lin, and Tuesday's Netflix Top 10 for Oct. 12–18.
