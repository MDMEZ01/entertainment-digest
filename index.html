📈 Today’s Deep Dive

The IMF Puts a Number on the AI Bubble Risk

Why it matters
  • The IMF’s Oct. 13 Global Financial Stability Report names an AI selloff as a top near-term risk, alongside a sovereign bond-yield spike and cyber threats.
  • It counts 14 hyperscaler-led “circular” deals since 2023: about $300B of equity and $650B of debt tied to roughly $1.4T in promised future revenue.
  • Its “wrong-way risk” warning: the stakes and the loans could lose value at the same moment the revenue they depend on dries up.
The money
  • Hyperscalers now account for 10% of global investment-grade bond issuance, up from under 1% in 2024.
  • Alphabet’s Ruth Porat told Fortune’s MPW Summit it will spend about $200B this year and more in 2027.
Historical context
  • In the late-1990s telecom boom, Lucent and Nortel lent customers money to buy their own gear, and those loans soured in the 2000–02 bust as the Nasdaq fell about 78% from its March 2000 peak.
Watch for
  • In the IMF’s unwind scenario, global GDP falls up to 0.4% by 2028; TSMC’s Oct. 15 earnings and Anthropic’s Oct. 14 investor day are the next demand tests.
