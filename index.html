📈 Today's Deep Dive

Anthropic's S-1: A Record-Scale IPO Arrives With an $80B Paper Loss

Why it matters
  • Anthropic made its S-1 public Monday for a Nasdaq listing under "ANT," led by Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley, with the week of Nov. 16 targeted.
  • First-half 2026 revenue hit $16.3B versus $1.2B a year earlier, and customers paying $100K+ rose to 11,900 from 950, with net revenue retention near 900%.
  • The filing landed the same day the Washington Post reported that Cathedral's AI agents got inside Anthropic's internal tools.
The money
  • The WSJ says the raise could reach $100B or more at a $1.5T–$2T valuation; the first-half net loss of $80.7B includes about $72B of non-cash convertible-note charges.
  • Anthropic lists $245B of minimum compute commitments (up to $114.5B with Google, at least $110B with Amazon) against $112.4B of cash at June 30.
Historical context
  • Saudi Aramco's 2019 listing raised about $29.4B including the greenshoe, the record to date, and Facebook's 2012 debut raised $16B; a $100B deal would be more than three Aramcos.
Watch for
  • Pricing terms and share count, expected in about two weeks, and how much of the raise prepays compute.
  • Whether the FTC, EU AI Office and state AG probes advance before the Nov. 16 listing week.
