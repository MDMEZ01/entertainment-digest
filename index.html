📈 Today’s Deep Dive

Is Paramount Litigating Politics in a Case With No Jury?

Why It Matters
  • Puck's Eriq Gardner questions why Paramount keeps escalating a public campaign — David Ellison's NYT op-ed, Ari Emanuel and Chris Wallace op-eds, and now a threatened California exit — despite the trial being a bench proceeding before Judge Araceli Martínez-Olguín with no jury to sway.
  • Constantly emphasizing that Martínez-Olguín is a Biden appointee risks irritating the very judge who will decide the case, and casting the suit as partisan could harden the 12-state coalition rather than fracture it.
  • Gardner suggests Paramount may be fighting two cases at once: one in federal court, and another for legitimacy within Hollywood, given SAG-AFTRA's opposition and the industry-wide debate over who should control CNN and Warner's IP.
The Money
  • Discovery is expected to reach AMC, Cinemark, Disney, Universal and Netflix, testing whether the states' market-definition theory accounts for how streaming has reshaped competition in exhibition and cable.
  • The UK's newly closed merger review offers a template: Paramount agreed to preserve Channel 5 News's editorial independence for five years, keep distinct linear/on-demand editorial identities, and accept an ongoing compliance-monitoring regime — "behavioral-plus" remedies short of a full asset sale.
Historical Context
  • Gardner points to Live Nation-Ticketmaster, where behavioral remedies that looked adequate at signing later became, in enforcers' eyes, evidence the underlying structural problem was never fixed — explaining why today's antitrust officials generally prefer structural remedies to conduct rules.
Watch For
  • Whether Paramount pivots from the public "weaponization of antitrust" framing toward its potentially stronger economic argument: that streaming has upended decades-old market-share presumptions.
  • Whether Ellison offers a symbolic concession, such as Emanuel's proposed CNN/CBS editorial oversight board, visible enough for Bonta's coalition to call a win while Ellison still keeps both companies intact.
