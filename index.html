📈 Today's Deep Dive

The Emmys Leave Broadcast: Awards Shows Follow the Audience to Streaming

Why it matters
  • Amazon's six-year global deal moves the Emmys to Prime Video starting with the 79th ceremony in September 2027, ending a broadcast run that dates to 1955.
  • The ABC/CBS/Fox/NBC "Wheel Deal" rotation ends, and with the Oscars moving to YouTube in 2029, broadcast is losing its two marquee awards franchises.
  • The show stays free to watch without a Prime subscription, signaling Amazon wants reach and ad inventory rather than sign-ups.
The money
  • Variety reports the deal is worth about $15M a year, against an audience that has fallen from a peak above 20M to about 7M.
Historical context
  • It mirrors Amazon's 2022 move of "Thursday Night Football" to an exclusive Prime Video package, which turned a broadcast staple into a streaming tentpole.
Watch for
  • Whether Amazon's Mike Hopkins ("We're on the clock, we gotta get rolling") brings the Emmys' ratings back up on a streaming platform.
  • Whether other awards shows follow the Emmys and Oscars to streaming as their broadcast deals expire.
