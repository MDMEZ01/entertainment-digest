📈 Today's Deep Dive

OpenAI's $20B Revenue Gap Exposes AI's Circular Money

Why it matters
  • The FT reported OpenAI told investors its annualized revenue is "approaching $50 billion," about $20B below the figure circulated last month.
  • Chip stocks sold off Oct. 8: Nvidia fell 2.94% to $230.48, the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped as much as 4% and the Nasdaq lost ~1.25%.
  • Fortune's Diane Brady notes Anthropic counts cloud-partner sales, so its $60B run-rate through July is "probably about a third less" on OpenAI's basis.
The money
  • Broadcom has reportedly discussed ~$30B of debt to help OpenAI buy jointly developed chips, after a similar $35B Anthropic deal.
  • Nvidia lined up six finance firms to mobilize $500B+ for customers, and AMD offered OpenAI and Meta warrants for up to 320M shares at a penny each.
Historical context
  • In 1999–2000 Lucent and Nortel lent billions to telecom carriers to buy their gear, and took heavy losses when those customers collapsed.
Watch for
  • Whether OpenAI's IPO, now pushed to early 2027, comes with revenue figures investors can reconcile.
  • Brady's test for any AI partner: "what capital is firmly committed, who bears the losses."
