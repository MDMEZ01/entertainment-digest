Nvidia's First Week Without Huang: Succession Search Meets Earnings Day

Why it matters
  • Nvidia lost $573 billion in market value last week, per Business Insider, more than all but 18 S&P 500 companies are worth.
  • Huang had 60 direct reports and Nvidia had no defined succession plan, so the board is now recruiting under a spotlight.
  • The FT reported the board approached Arm CEO Rene Haas, a seven-year Nvidia veteran; Arm shares fell 6.5% Friday.
The money
  • NVDA closed Friday at $162.85, down 12.75% on the week and about 32% below its Oct. 6 record of $239.24.
  • Tuesday's Q3 consensus is $109.2B in revenue and $2.47 EPS, with interim CEO Colette Kress leading the call; HSBC cut its target to $170 from $365.
Historical context
  • Apple pushed out Steve Jobs in 1985 and brought him back in 1997; Dick Costolo is already predicting Nvidia will 'bring Jensen back' after a missed quarter or two.
Watch for
  • Whether guidance on Tuesday holds up without Huang, and whether a 'Stand with Jensen' petition near 100,000 signatures moves the board.
  • APEC in Shenzhen Nov. 18–19, where U.S.–China chip export policy will be tested in the scandal's wake.
